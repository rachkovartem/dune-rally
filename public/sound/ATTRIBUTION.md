# Sound attribution

Engine loops in `forester/` and `pajero/` are short cuts (2-3 s, some pitch-flattened) from freesound.org HQ previews.
Cuts, start times and estimated rpm are in `manifest.json`. Tyre, wind, scrape and turbo sounds are generated in code.
